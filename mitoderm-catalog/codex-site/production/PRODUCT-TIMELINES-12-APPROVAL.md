## Canonical film / timing rule

The separate approved **V-TECH SYSTEM** reference (not modified) has 873 frames / 24 fps = **36.375 s** and **10.35 viewport heights** of controlled scroll. Other durations below are deliberate *proposals based on each action*, not durations of completed films. Scroll controls time; it is not fixed-rate auto-play.

**Formula:** `video_position_seconds = scene_video_in + ((scroll_vh - scene_vh_in) / scene_vh_duration) × scene_duration_seconds`. One vh is one actual viewport height; 1.2 vh on a 900px desktop is 1080px of native scrolling.
