- **EXO-NAD**: the supplied `NAD.png` is champagne/gold/ivory. No #1E4634 BIOSPICULE green in page sections. The turquoise LONGEVITY SERUM bottle is a physical product accent, not permission to repaint the whole VM page cyan.
- **V-TECH / EXOTECH GEL / EXOSIGNAL / EXOCELL** share VM family identity but retain their OWN real pack colors (gold, black, turquoise).
- **BIOSPICULE**: products have cream/white bases with deep-forest label; digitally #1E4634 is useful for background and gold is a restrained accent.
- **MITOTECH**: print Pantone 3435 C and 3375 C remain authoritative; screen HEX values here are only approximate, not validated Pantone conversions.
- **Devices**: silver/charcoal with accurate hardware color and real UI.
- No generative color grading that makes foil change from champagne to orange/green or alters printed lettering. Compare one physical material under one calibrated light setup.
