**Product fidelity:** box, sachet, mask = OUR renders `site/design/exocell-3d/d1-meet.jpg`…`d6-protocol.jpg` and alpha layers
`site/design/exocell-3d/layers/` (`d1-meet-box.png`, `d5-burst-sachets.png`, `d3-maskout-mask.png`), `mask-layers.png`. The box
turns only between its front and left side (others unphotographed); the box never opens on screen (no photo); the sachet shows
only its front. The mask ON the face must be generated: check its outline (eye holes, nose flap, mouth, slit at the right jaw)
against `d3-maskout-mask.png` in every key still. PACKAGING: matte muted-teal portrait carton, dark navy print: VM monogram,
'VM corporation', 'EXOCELL' ('EXO' bold) / letter-spaced 'M A S K', light italic 'Your Second Skin' / 'For Exo Bio-Regeneration';
left panel 'EXOCELL MASK' vertical, grey. Sachet: turquoise foil, rounded corners, tear notch top-left, VM, 'EXOCELL' / 'M A S K',
'1 face mask', '25 ml'. Mask: translucent milky-white bio-cellulose with a faint aqua tint, double (white + pale blue layer).
**Extra negative (all shots):** `no fibres, threads or fabric weave in the mask, no blue-dyed skin, no blood, no wounds, no
glowing or retouched skin, the redness never disappears by itself, no clock in frame, the box never opens`.
