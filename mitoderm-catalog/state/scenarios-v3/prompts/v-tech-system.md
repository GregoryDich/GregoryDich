Companion of `src/v-tech-system.md` (the Hebrew chapter is the contract: shots, scroll ranges, approved texts). Shared bible =
`prompts/exo-nad.md` §0 (cast `cast-C01`, room `set-R01`, look, shared negative, aspect plan, MODEL classes): paste it on top
of every prompt. Here the line accent is GOLD `#c9a24b` (tunic piping, haze around the product). MODEL = `per costs.json → <class>`
(costs.json not on disk at writing time; fill exact id + params before the first paid run; price + balance, run only after "approved").
Aspect: master 16:9 1920×1080 → upscale ×2; every shot with people re-generated 9:16 from its own vertical still; product-only
shots cropped. Each shot ≤ 5 s generated, trimmed to the listed length.

**Product fidelity:** box/tray/vials/sachets = OUR renders `site/design/vtech-3d/hd/v1-meet.webp`…`v6-kit.webp` and alpha
layers `site/design/vtech-3d/layers/*.png` (re-render 3840 with `src/render-vtech.cjs`); MITOPEN = `site/proto/scenes/mitopen.js`
(`site/public/mitopen/img/still.webp`). Method (a) composite into stills, (b) i2v from composited frames + `replace-object` with our
render as reference, (c) three.js motion over the plate. PACKAGING (never retyped by the generator): tall mirror-gold carton,
black bands top/bottom, silver 'VM', black V-with-triangle, 'V-TECH' / 'S Y S T E M'; amber glass vials, flip-off cap (white on
packshot, silver on photos — ask), white V mark + 'V-TECH' + ticks; gold foil sachets, small white nozzle, 'V-TECH' / 'GEL MASK'.
**Extra negative (all shots):** `no blood, no wounds, no bruising, no glowing or retouched skin, no needle near the eyes, no
dermaroller, no extra devices, the redness never disappears between shots`.
