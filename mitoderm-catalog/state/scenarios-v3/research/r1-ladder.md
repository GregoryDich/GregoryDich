## 1. Ben Hunt's Awareness Ladder

Source: Ben Hunt, "Convert! Designing Web Sites to Increase Traffic and Conversion", Wiley, 2011 (book chapter
"Working Through the Awareness Ladder", section "Consider Step 0"). Listing: https://www.schweitzer-online.de/buch/Hunt/Convert/9780470616338/B359325/
Summaries used: https://medium.com/agile-pies/ben-hunts-awareness-ladder-104d872a3d20 ,
https://dpigraphics.net/all-users-are-not-created-equal/ , https://www.keywordsblogger.com/tools/the-awareness-ladder-how-to-set-up-your-website-for-maximum-conversions/ ,
https://rband.pro/en/useful/pro-marketing/ben-hunts-ladder , https://www.sostav.ru/blogs/278345/53249

IMPORTANT: Hunt's ladder is SIX rungs numbered 0-5 (Step 0 to Step 5), not five. BRIEF.md D2 uses five rungs (1-5);
that matches Schwartz, not Hunt. Recommendation: keep our five working rungs but state in the client file that Hunt's Step 0
is folded into our rung 1 ("no problem" and "problem" are both handled by the first screen).

| Hunt step | Visitor state (secondary-source wording) | What the page must do |
|---|---|---|
| 0 No problem | does not see a need | introduce the problem and why it matters; not a sales pitch; story or fact, not product |
| 1 Problem | feels the problem | confirm it is real for them, signal that solutions exist |
| 2 Solutions exist | knows kinds of solutions | educate on solution types, then steer toward your approach |
| 3 Your solution | knows the category, not your product | "lay out very clearly the BENEFITS of the product" (paraphrase of Hunt); avoid open attacks on competitors |
| 4 Benefits | knows benefits, not yet persuaded | build "the vision in their mind of how much easier their life will be"; answer fears with other buyers' experience: reviews, cases |
| 5 Convinced | ready | make action easy; only competence demos and details matter now |

Mechanics (consensus of the summaries):
- Hunt: most sites target about Step 3, yet large profitable audiences sit at Steps 2, 1 and even 0
  (https://searchenginewatch.com/?p=19917 as summarised in search results).
- One page = one target rung. Mismatch fails: asking for the sale at Step 0-1 repels, over-educating Step 5 delays.
- Move a visitor ONE rung per block: each block states where the reader is and ends with a bridge to the next rung.
- Length is [inference] proportional to distance to travel: low rungs need story and more space, rung 5 needs a form, not prose.
- Proof: needed from rung 4 (cases, other buyers); at rung 5 proof is reassurance (guarantee, contact, who answers).
- Hunt pages that serve several rungs let the visitor jump: lead with the lowest rung, keep a visible action for the highest.
  This is exactly the owner's "form from block 1".

### Relation to Schwartz's five stages
Eugene Schwartz, "Breakthrough Advertising" (1966): Unaware, Problem Aware, Solution Aware, Product Aware, Most Aware.
Summaries: https://www.shortform.com/blog/customer-awareness/ , https://aokmarketing.com/the-five-stages-of-awareness-and-how-to-move-people-one-step/ ,
https://segwise.ai/blog/advertising-awareness-levels-2026 . Hunt built on Schwartz (sostav.ru article above).
Per summaries: Unaware = lead with a startling fact or story, not a pitch; Problem aware = name the problem; Solution aware =
echo the emotion, make your product the "inevitable" solution; Product aware = answer remaining doubts (proof, results,
difference) [inference, sources thin]; Most aware = offer, price, deadline. Warning: an offer ad shown to unaware people is ignored.

Mapping used for MITODERM (our rungs R1-R5):
| Ours | Schwartz | Hunt | Meaning for a cosmetologist |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Unaware / Problem aware | 0-1 | "my clients' hair/skin issue is my growth gap" |
| R2 | Solution aware | 2 | "there are professional approaches beyond what I do" |
| R3 | Solution -> Product aware | 3 | "this product, this mechanism, this look and feel" |
| R4 | Product aware | 4 | "it works, other cosmetologists got results" |
| R5 | Most aware | 5 | "how do I start, what do I get" -> form |

## 2. Building love for a product

- Know-Like-Trust: Bob Burg, "Endless Referrals": "All things being equal, people will do business with, and refer business to,
  those people they know, like, and trust." Source: https://thinkadvisor.com/2012/03/09/the-know-like-and-trust-factor
  (attribution disputed in other sources; I found no proof Chris Brogan coined it). One source: know and like come fast online,
  trust is slow. Fit: Know = R1-R3, Like = the film's emotion, Trust = R4 cases.
- Lovemarks, Kevin Roberts, "Lovemarks: The Future Beyond Brands" (2004): a brand wins love AND respect through
  Mystery (stories, myths, icons, dreams, secret formulas), Sensuality (five senses touchpoints), Intimacy (commitment, empathy,
  belonging, personal relevance). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lovemark ; critique (Dan Herman): creating the three does not
  guarantee love, https://www.themanager.org/?p=96 . Fit for the film: Mystery = ingredient origin and the hero pack's entrance;
  Sensuality = texture, sound of opening, hands (macro shots); Intimacy = the cosmetologist-client moment and "family MITODERM".
- Story arc [inference from Hunt+Roberts]: curiosity -> discovery -> wow -> trust (real case) -> desire -> action -> belonging.
  This is the owner's love arc already in BRIEF D2.
- What makes professionals adopt a product. I found NO quantitative study isolating this for aesthetic skincare.
  Indications only: a 2026 JCAD review on adoption stresses perceived risk, peer and supervising-physician norms, and combining
  trial with real-world evidence (https://jcadonline.com/wp-content/uploads/J-Clin-Aesthet-Dermatol.-2026199-10-Suppl-1-S14-S16.pdf,
  dermatology therapies, not skincare); Kline & Company (paywalled, old): nearly all of 300 dermatologists/plastic surgeons/spa
  managers wanted more samples, training and educational material (via https://www.cosmeticsandtoiletries.com/formulating/category/skincare/6831797.html);
  a later survey: over 80% of spas and physicians rank samples as the best support (same search batch); practitioners say they
  recommend products with clinical data (https://www.theaestheticguide.com/aesthetic-guide/science-backed-skincare-reigns-2022).
  Practical levers for MITODERM [inference]: real before/after with consent; training and certificate; sample or starter set;
  peer cosmetologists on camera; events and community; business case for the clinic (new service line, retention, margin) with
  no invented numbers.

## 3. Lead capture from the first screen

- Sticky CTA: one case study, a mobile sticky bottom bar lifted homepage conversion +20.4% (95.1% probability); author warns it
  helps when the decision happens below the first CTA, i.e. long pages - ours https://convertibles.dev/blogs/case-studies/homepage-sticky-cta-case-study
  (single vendor test, not a meta-analysis; no rigorous study on scroll-triggered vs always-on).
- Short forms: widely cited 11 -> 4 fields = +120% (Imagescape) - original not located, unverified. Use 3 fields anyway.
- WhatsApp in Israel: penetration 90% (Infobip 2026) to 98-99% (JPost / Israel Internet Association citations); treated as the
  default business channel (Times of Israel blogs by Achiya Cohen). https://www.infobip.com/blog/whatsapp-statistics-2025-global-usage-market-overview ,
  https://blogs.timesofisrael.com/how-whatsapp-runs-israeli-small-business/ . No Israeli click-to-chat conversion data found.
- B2B beauty conversion drivers: not found as a measured benchmark; use the adoption levers in section 2 as offers.
- Scroll-driven film: pre-rendered frame sequence tied to scroll position inside a pinned (sticky) stage in a tall container;
  keep the real product central; compress (WebP); static fallback for reduced motion; apple.com now leans video scrubbing,
  frames still win on photographic smoothness (blog-level sources: https://ddingpy.github.io/mynote/posts/building-an-apple-style-scroll-experience-like-the-macbook-neo-page ,
  https://www.framer.com/community/marketplace/components/scroll-sequences/).

Pattern for MITODERM: persistent CTA (mobile bottom bar, desktop header button) -> side panel with 3 fields (name, phone,
"I am": cosmetologist / clinic owner / doctor / other; product prefilled) + WhatsApp button as equal path; repeat the same
CTA text in-page only at R4->R5 and the end. Do not use a modal on load.

## 4. Proposal for MITODERM

Home (one page, rungs R1-R2, route selection):
1. H1 hero: 3D lineup/pack hanging, moves with scroll (as now). Rung R1. Sticky CTA already present: "leave details".
2. Problem choice (hair / dull skin / relief and scars / recovery / devices) or by name. Rung R1-R2: the visitor names their
   client's problem. Hotspot click starts the 2-4 s transition clip.
3. Short "one film, 13 products" strip with family and events teaser (R4-R5 shortcut for those already convinced).
4. Footer form (same 3 fields).

Product page (film in the cabinet; one rung per block, scroll-frames):
| Block | Rung | Content | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 Transition end frame | R1 | pack lands on the table | continuity with home |
| 1 Client in chair | R1 | the concern, gently | problem named by a human, not a pitch |
| 2 Pack arrives, opened | R2-R3 | texture, mechanism in one sentence | approved texts only |
| 3 Ritual of application | R3 | hands, close-ups | no protocols |
| 4 Real before/after | R4 | wipe, caption "after N sessions - individual results" | owner photos only |
| 5 Client delighted, cosmetologist proud | R4 | emotion, love peak | |
| 6 "You can be this cosmetologist" | R5 | offer choices + form | owner picks offers; no invented prices |
| 7 Family and events | R5 | belonging, events | dates from owner |
| 8 Info below | - | product facts, link to protocols | for the already-convinced |

Role of the clinic film: it is the emotional spine (Sensuality + Intimacy + Mystery) that carries R1-R4 in 30-40 s; the
cosmetologist hero is the viewer's proxy; the final line turns "I admire" into "I can". Form position: always reachable
(sticky), explicitly opened at block 6, repeated at the bottom.

Open questions for owner: real before/after per product (consent); event dates and what "family" includes; which offers
(training, starter set, sample, partner status) are real; WhatsApp business number.
