MICRONEEDLING & BIOMICRONEEDLING

## 1. V-TECH SERUM + MITOPEN

### Step 1 – Skin cleansing
On clean, dry skin, after washing and cleansing the face.

### Step 2 – V-TECH SERUM + MITOPEN
Draw up V-TECH Serum into a syringe using a draw-up needle – according to the treated area: face and neck approx. 1.5 mL.
Apply the serum with a microneedling device: MITOPEN.
Working depth is determined by the device, the treated area, skin thickness and professional assessment.
The treatment is performed in combination: first drip the V-TECH, then microneedling. Divide by areas.
Begin with the stamping technique, then complete with the crosshatch technique.
It is important to reach erythema.
Do not use acids without a neutralizer.

### Additional combinations
RF | RF MICRONEEDLING | CO2 LASER

### Recommended dosage
Per treatment area – face / neck / décolleté:
• V-TECH Serum: up to 2 mL
• V-TECH Gel Mask: up to 2 mL

### Step 3 – V-TECH GEL MASK
After completing the work with the serum, apply V-TECH Gel Mask to the same area.
Massage gently until fully absorbed. No rinsing required.

### Step 4 – Finishing
It is recommended to apply EXOCELL MASK to deliver hyaluronic acid, plant extracts and aloe vera.

### Step 5 – Hydration and protection
DERMA RECOVERY CREAM
Sun protection factor is mandatory

### Frequency and treatment series
One treatment every 14 days.
Recommended series: 4–6 treatments, depending on skin condition and treatment goal.
Base protocol of 4 consecutive treatments: every two weeks
After completing the series, one maintenance treatment every two to three months is recommended, depending on skin condition, in order to preserve and maximize the results of the treatment series.

### Storage after opening
Use the ampoule within one month of opening, in accordance with the product storage instructions.

## BIOSPICULE™ MICRO BOOST 10% + V-TECH

### Step 1 – Skin cleansing
Cleanse the skin; do not scrub aggressively. At the end of cleansing, dry the skin completely.

### Step 2 – Applying MICRO BOOST 10% and controlled massage
Spread MICRO BOOST 10% gradually over the working areas and massage with even, controlled pressure, according to the training technique, until the product is absorbed.
Massage intensity affects the intensity of irritation: the more intensive the pressure and massage, the more significant the skin reaction and the longer the recovery period may be, including peeling.
Therefore, adjust the massage intensity, pressure and manner of work to the skin condition, skin type, professional assessment, treatment goal and desired effect.
Work in a controlled manner and also take the patient's/client's comfort during the treatment into account.

### Step 3 – Combining active ingredients
After MICRO BOOST 10% has been absorbed, depending on the selected protocol, active ingredients may be chosen such as:
• V-TECH Serum + V-TECH Gel Mask
• MITOTECH CELL BOOSTER
• LONGEVITY SERUM

### Step 4 – Finishing the treatment
• EXOCELL MASK as a finishing and restorative mask
• DERMA RECOVERY CREAM to complete hydration, nourishment and support of the skin barrier
• SPF sunscreen of at least 30 is mandatory

### Treatment series
A series of 3–4 treatments may be performed, with 4–6 weeks between treatments.
After completing the series, a maintenance treatment may be performed according to the assessment of skin condition, treatment goals and the patient's/client's response.

### Home care:
For home use, the BIOSPICULE home series for renewal, EXOTECH Gel for optimal hydration and RECOVERY Cream for nourishment and intensive hydration may be recommended.

### Expected skin reaction
Due to the high concentration of spicules, a characteristic tingling sensation may appear during the treatment and in the following days, mainly on contact with the skin.
• Temporary redness
• Mild swelling
• Sensation of warmth
• Dryness
• Feeling of tightness
• Sensitivity to touch
• Peeling of varying degree
The intensity of the reaction varies between patients and is not in itself an indicator of treatment efficacy. It may be influenced by skin type, sensitivity level, quantity of product and massage technique.
Spicules may remain in the outer layers of the skin for up to 72 hours.

## 3. EXOSIGNAL HAIR Professional scalp treatment protocol
MITOPEN + EXOSIGNAL HAIR

### Step 1 – Preparing and cleansing the treatment area
Cleanse the scalp area thoroughly and remove residues of creams, serums, sebum or other substances.
Disinfection may be performed with a disinfectant suitable for skin, in accordance with the clinic's professional working protocol.
The MITOSCAN camera may be used to examine the scalp, the condition of the follicles, thinning and inflammation.

### Step 2 – Drying
Dry the treatment area completely.

### Step 3 – EXOSIGNAL HAIR SERUM
Use approx. 2 mL of the serum, depending on the size of the treated area.
The treatment is performed in combination with MITOPEN.
Working depth: above 0.25 mm, depending on the area, scalp condition and professional assessment.
Proceed simultaneously with the product and the device. Divide the work into areas: first drip a little of the product onto a given area, then perform stamping.

### Step 4 – Serum absorption
After the work, gently massage the scalp with gloves until the product is fully absorbed.
Indication – stamping and red dots indicate a successful treatment.

### Frequency and treatment series
• One treatment every 14 days.
• At least 4 consecutive treatments are recommended.
• After completing the series, one maintenance treatment every two months may be performed.
Frequency and number of treatments may vary according to the degree of hair loss, scalp condition, professional assessment and the patient's/client's response.
Recommend using a daily spray every evening on a clean scalp to improve and balance the scalp.

### Storage
After opening, store the ampoule refrigerated. Stability after opening: up to one month.

## 4. EXO-NAD SKIN LONGEVITY PEEL

### Protocol and method of application
The treatment consists of three steps: EXO BIPHASIC PEEL | pH NORMALIZER | LONGEVITY SERUM

### Step 1 – EXO BIPHASIC PEEL
Shake the biphasic solution well before use.
Option 1 – thin, sensitive or somewhat mature skin:
Draw up 1 mL of the solution and apply evenly to the treatment area with gentle massage. Leave the solution on the skin for up to 20 minutes, depending on skin condition and sensitivity.
Option 2 – thick or more mature skin:
Draw up 1 mL of the solution, apply to the treatment area with massage and leave for 15 minutes. Then draw up another 1 mL, spread over the same area with gentle massage and leave for up to 15 additional minutes. Total exposure time: up to 30 minutes.

### Step 2 – pH NORMALIZER
Draw up approx. 2 mL of the neutralizer and apply evenly over the entire area treated with EXO PEEL.
Leave on the skin for 2–3 minutes. Then rinse thoroughly with water and dry the skin completely.

### Step 3 – LONGEVITY SERUM
Draw up approx. 2 mL of the Longevity Serum and apply evenly to the treatment area.
Allow the serum to absorb into the skin. Do not rinse.

### Storage and use after opening
After opening, the ampoules may be used for one month; store in a cool place and in accordance with the storage instructions. The peeling can be used for 3 years.
The kit allows up to 15 treatments, depending on the quantity used per treatment.
Change gloves, needles and syringes between steps.

## 6. Home care instructions after in-clinic treatments

### The first 24 hours
Do not wash or wet the treatment area. Avoid sports or any activity that causes significant sweating. Avoid, as far as possible, touching and rubbing the treatment area, and avoid makeup. Hygiene and cleanliness: do not use makeup brushes / sponges or devices for at least 3 days.

### Hydration and recovery
Continue at home with suitable hydration and nourishment, according to the practitioner's instructions. It is recommended to use DERMA RECOVERY CREAM to support hydration and the skin barrier.

### UV protection
Use SPF 30 sunscreen at least every morning, and it is recommended to avoid direct sun exposure as far as possible for at least 5 days.

### Heat, water and activity
Avoid sauna, hot showers, swimming and activity that causes significant sweating for 48 hours. Allow optimal absorption of the active ingredients.

### Active ingredients
Do not use acids, retinoids, benzoyl peroxide, scrub exfoliants or irritating products for 5–7 days before and after the treatment, or until the skin has returned to its normal condition and feel. Avoid products containing alcohol or fragrance.
Melanin suppressor as needed.

### Peeling and sensations after treatment
Tingling, redness, a sensation of warmth, dryness or mild peeling may appear after the treatment.
Do not scratch, rub or deliberately remove peeling skin.
In case of significant pain, unusual swelling, blisters, persistent rash or any unexpected reaction, contact the practitioner and refer for medical evaluation as needed.
With spongilla, the sensation of needles may remain for up to 72 hours.

Follow-up and complementary routine – a gentle skincare routine may be resumed gradually approx. 3–5 days after the treatment.

### Home maintenance protocol:
In the evening, the BIOSPICULE 1.5% or 2.5% series may be used for renewal and maintaining results, with Recovery Cream over it for hydration.
EXOTECH Gel may be used to maintain the exosome results immediately after a biomicroneedling series.
